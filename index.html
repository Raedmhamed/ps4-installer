Raed PS4 Web-Only Installer

Enter Your PS4 IP Address

Go to PS4 Settings > Network > View Connection Status to find it


How to use:
1. Make sure GoldHEN is running on your PS4.
2. Ensure the Remote Package Installer feature is enabled in your GoldHEN settings.
3. Enter your console's network IP address above and click the button.
4. Exit the browser and check Notifications > Downloads on the PS4 menu.
